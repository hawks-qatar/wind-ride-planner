How the score works

For each stretch of every route we work out which way you are riding and when you will be there (at a steady average speed from the start of the ride window), then look up the forecast wind for that place and hour.

  • Headwind out, tailwind home is best. You fight the wind while fresh and get pushed home when tired. The reverse costs points.
  • Crosswinds cost the most, and count extra on open desert, coast and causeways where there is no shelter.
  • Gusts over km/h cost points; average wind over km/h costs points.
  • Skip or ride short if the wind is over km/h or gusts over km/h.
  • Heat over °C and low visibility (dust, haze or fog) are flagged.

Scores go from 0 to 100. Safe routes are always listed before unsafe ones.

Forecast: Open-Meteo. Route waypoints are approximate — always check the road and conditions yourself. Ride safe.
